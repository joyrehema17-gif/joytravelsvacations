Meet Joy Rehema

Welcome to Joy Travels and Vacations! My name is Joy Rehema, and I am passionate about sharing the beauty of Kenya with travelers from around the world. Every safari and holiday we organize is carefully planned to create unforgettable memories, whether you're dreaming of witnessing the Great Migration, relaxing on the white sandy beaches of Watamu, or experiencing authentic Kenyan culture. "I believe every journey should create memories that last a lifetime. Whether it's your first safari or your tenth visit to Kenya, I would be honored to help you discover our country's incredible wildlife, culture, and coastline. I look forward to welcoming you to Kenya!"

My mission is simple: to provide safe, personalized, affordable and memorable travel experiences with genuine Kenyan hospitality.
